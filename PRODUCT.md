# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React + Vite, Tailwind CSS, Motion (Framer Motion), GSAP

## Users
Local residents of Asansol, college students, families, party organizers, commuters, and vehicle buyers seeking trusted used motorcycles, scooters, cars, and a welcoming dining lounge.

## Product Purpose
A contemporary, approachable, and memorable multi-page website for RK HIVE representing two established local Asansol businesses along Shakespeare Sarani: **Hive The Lounge RK** (dining, mocktails, celebrations) and **Hive RK Enterprise Automobiles** (pre-owned two-wheelers, cars, and vehicle exchange).

## Positioning
An authentic local destination in Asansol offering honest, value-driven hospitality and trusted automotive services under a unified contemporary brand identity — grounded in reality, not artificial luxury.

## Operating Context
Explored on mobile and desktop by customers looking for table reservations, menu items, party bookings, used bike/car inventory, pricing transparency, and direct WhatsApp contact with business owners.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Dual-brand homepage with clear, honest navigation to The Lounge and Automobile Centre.
- The Lounge: real menu highlights (Chinese, Mughlai, North Indian, Mocktails, Snacks), party space booking, verified Justdial/Google review highlights (~4.1/5 stars).
- Automobile Centre: real vehicle categories (Royal Enfield, commuter bikes, scooters, family cars), exchange & financing inquiry, documentation assistance.
- Direct WhatsApp integration with pre-filled inquiries for fast local response.
- Clear distinction between confirmed facts and items requiring owner confirmation.
- Authentic, realistic photography and transparent placeholders.

## Brand Commitments
- Business Names: Hive The Lounge RK 2.0 & Hive RK Enterprise Automobiles
- Location: Shakespeare Sarani, Jyotinagar, Near Blessed Hospital & Gitanjali Dhaba, Asansol, West Bengal – 713305
- Palette: Warm terracotta/amber & forest charcoal for the lounge; industrial slate & steel blue for automobiles; clean off-white background with contemporary warmth (no excessive gold or fake billionaire aesthetics).

## Evidence on Hand
- Justdial listing for Hive The Lounge RK (~4.1 stars, 450+ reviews).
- Justdial and Instagram listings for Hive RK Enterprise Automobiles (Jyotinagar, Shakespeare Sarani).
- Verified local menu items, pricing band (~₹500 for two), and service offerings.

## Product Principles
1. Grounded & Authentic: Avoid fake hypercars, imaginary caviar, and artificial prestige. Celebrate the real local business.
2. Distinctive Contemporary Editorial Design: High craft layout, distinctive typography, and rich visual interest without resorting to generic templates or tacky gradients.
3. Purposeful Motion: Smooth image reveals, scroll-triggered cues, and micro-interactions that elevate the feel while keeping performance snappy.
4. Clear Local Utility: Phone, WhatsApp, directions to Shakespeare Sarani, and transparent booking inquiries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast compliant typography, mobile-first touch ergonomics, semantic markup, and `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks.
